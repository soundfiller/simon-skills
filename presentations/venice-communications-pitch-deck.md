### Slide 4 — The Economics That Changed
**Mode:** Dark  
**Type:** Framework  
**Action Title:** Coordination cost is collapsing toward zero. That changes everything.

**Content:**
- For 150 years, the firm existed to solve one problem: coordination cost
  - You hired people because coordinating external talent was too expensive
  - You built departments because information couldn't flow any other way
  - You had 16 people because that's how many brains you could afford to coordinate
- AI collapses coordination cost
  - Information retrieval: from hours to seconds
  - Cross-project knowledge: from impossible to instant
  - Multi-stakeholder alignment: from meetings to automated synthesis
- The firm persists as an *accountability shell* — not a coordination machine
- **Implication:** A 16-person agency can now deliver the output of a 50–100 person agency
  - Not by working harder. By working differently
- **The Headless Firm (Mantix, 2026):** Coordination cost shifts from O(n²) to O(n) — from topology-dominated to throughput-dominated
  - Every new capability no longer requires another coordination layer. It plugs into the protocol
  - This is the economic force behind solo-founders (36.3% of new ventures) and small agencies outperforming large ones
